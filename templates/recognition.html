How Pretrained Deep Learning Facial Recognition Works (Presentation Quick Reference)

1

Face Detection (YuNet)

A fast, lightweight convolutional neural network detects the bounding box and 5 key facial landmarks (left/right eyes, nose tip, left/right mouth corners) regardless of slight tilt.

2

Affine Alignment & Normalization

The detected face is geometrically rotated and cropped into a standardized 112×112 pixel canvas so lighting and pose variations are minimized.

3

Embedding Extraction (SFace)

A deep CNN maps the aligned face into an irreversible 128-dimensional mathematical vector where faces of the same person are clustered close together.

4

Cosine Similarity & Attendance

The vector is matched against registered student encodings. If cosine similarity ≥ 0.45, identity is verified and attendance is saved with uniqueness check (no duplicates).
