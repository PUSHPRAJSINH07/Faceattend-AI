How AI Facial Recognition Works

1

Face Detection (YuNet)

A lightweight CNN detects the bounding box and 5 key facial landmarks regardless of slight tilt.

2

Affine Alignment

The face is geometrically aligned into a standardized 112×112 canvas to minimize lighting variation.

3

Embedding Extraction (SFace)

A deep CNN maps the aligned face into a 128-dimensional vector where same-person faces cluster together.

4

Cosine Similarity & Attendance

The vector is matched against registered encodings. If similarity ≥ {{ threshold }}, attendance is saved.
